OUR PROPOSED SERVICE BOUNDARIES

Legal & Client Data Policy.

Website repairs should not require access to a client's financial records. We intend to operate on a minimum-access basis: only the website permissions needed for authorized work.

FINANCIAL DATA PROTECTION

Our boundaries, in plain language.

01

Financial records stay outside our work

For standard website design and repair services, TMCOMTECH does not request clients' financial statements, bank records, accounting records or customers' full payment card information. Clients should not send this data to us.

02

Limited website access only

We aim to use specific, restricted website permissions and an agreed scope of work. If the offered access reveals unrelated sensitive records, we will request narrower access or decline the task.

03

No payment-processing repairs at launch

TMCOMTECH's initial website-repair services will exclude payment gateway administration, payment transactions and credit-card processing. A simple link or button repair will be evaluated for payment-system exposure before accepting it.

Written authorization & minimum access

Before work begins, the client and TMCOMTECH should document the website, requested changes and permissions allowed. Where practical, we prefer restricted editor or developer accounts, a staging environment, and non-sensitive test content instead of full administrative access.

We do not need or request a customer's online banking credentials, accounting passwords, payment processor dashboard access, financial statements or live cardholder details to perform ordinary site repairs. We will not ask clients to share them by email or chat.

If sensitive information becomes accessible

If financial or other sensitive information appears unexpectedly during a website assignment, our procedure is to stop the affected work, avoid inspecting or copying that information, notify the client through the agreed contact channel and arrange appropriate access restrictions before continuing. Necessary incident details should be recorded without copying the sensitive content itself.

We intend to keep website backups and test files within the agreed scope, avoid unnecessary copies of private client data, and use fictitious or properly sanitized data for testing. Client financial data must not be entered into AI tools as part of our standard repair workflow.

Excluded work & individual agreements

Our initial scope excludes payment processing, bank integrations, accounting systems, and handling live customer payment details. If a proposed job cannot be completed without those systems, TMCOMTECH will not take it on as a standard website repair.

Any future service involving different types of data or access would require a separate scope, appropriate safeguards and explicit client agreement. This page describes our intended practices for standard website projects; it is not a substitute for a project-specific agreement, full privacy notice or legal advice.

Policy status

We will review these procedures and service agreements before launching paid services. No website policy can eliminate all legal or security risk; actual permissions, systems and work performed must match the commitments described here.
